AHC TRAFFIC-LED COMMERCIAL IMPROVEMENTS — 12 OCTOBER 2026

DECISION
Prioritise the existing appliance guides that already attract search visitors.
Their commercial changes are too recent to judge from the current reporting
window. Improve the route from a sizing answer to a suitable product, then measure
reader referrals and merchant results before expanding the catalogue.

Search Console, 12 September–9 October vs 15 August–11 September:
151 vs 107 clicks (+41.1%); 23,477 vs 14,263 impressions (+64.6%).
CTR fell from approximately 0.750% to 0.643%. Aggregate average position improved

Do not label these totals qualified visits or verified human traffic.

No fresh merchant orders or commission were verified in this review.

HOW TO JUDGE THE CHANGES
Record 12 October as an intervention, not a clean baseline day. Compare equal
complete 14-day ranges: 28 September–11 October and 13–26 October, after reporting
settles. The earlier range includes the 10–11 October product additions, so this
is observational, not a controlled test or isolated estimate of today's impact.
Read each appliance page separately: search clicks; landing/engaged sessions
where measurable; non-QA affiliate events; and merchant clicks, ordered items,
earned commission and refunds. Explain attribution gaps and keep unknown human
status unknown. Never attribute shared-tag Amazon orders to a page cohort.
Traffic without retailer clicks suggests relevance/placement needs work; retailer
clicks without sales calls for offer, price, availability and attribution checks.
At these volumes, a week without an order does not establish a conversion rate.
Compare actual earned commission with costs before claiming commercial success.

The outdoor/pantry/small-bathroom trial pages were not changed by this work.
No artificial affiliate clicks, purchases, messages or new monitoring automation.
