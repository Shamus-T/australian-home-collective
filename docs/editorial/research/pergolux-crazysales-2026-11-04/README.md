# Louvred pergola research record — 4 November 2026

The resulting public guide is `/guides/louvred-pergolas-australia/`. It is independent planning and buying guidance, not a product review or a claim of installation/testing. The existing outdoor shade article links to it contextually. Both use the shared article layout and existing header.

No Pergolux or Crazy Sales product recommendation is approved by this review. NNE Living remains excluded. The buying guide retains an explicit primary-product gap in `article-commercial-coverage.json`; it is not marked commercially complete.

## Public evidence retained

- `variant-snapshot.json`: clean Australian storefront product JSON, including exact model/variant/SKU, listed price and availability flag. A storefront flag is not warehouse verification or postcode delivery assurance.
- `document-index.json`: official assembly portal documents, hashes and labelled dimension fields. Includes 42 drawings and 12 manuals across manual, Pro and Pro Max Series 4. Some dimensions are clear spans or other labelled distances; do not assume every `L` value is an outside footprint.
- `awning-document-index.json`: the manual and ten linked size drawings. The linked drawing identifiers include EU model codes while Australian storefront variants use AU codes; applicability remains unresolved.

Manufacturer source pages: [manual Series 4](https://pergolux.au/products/pergola), [Pro](https://pergolux.au/products/pergola-pro), [Pro Max](https://pergolux.au/products/pergola-pro-max), [awning](https://pergolux.au/products/awnings), [assembly portal](https://www.pergolux.app/assembly), [Australian warranty page](https://pergolux.au/pages/10-year-warranty).

## Publication boundary

The manufacturer material did not establish a reconciled current Australian model/size/configuration engineering and anchoring record. Wind claims are not repeated as verified ratings. Warranty summaries also require reconciliation with the dated Australian terms. The awning's English manual requires retraction in rain/frost and when unused; no rain-shelter recommendation is made. These are limits of the evidence obtained, not a finding that a product is unsafe.

Account approvals, commission exceptions, generated-link inspection, retailer fulfilment gaps and independent owner-feedback assessments are recorded in a separate private working report. No account-specific terms or tracking links are published here. Link generation establishes intended routing only; it is not an attribution test or earned commission.

The public guide instead cites Australian Government Your Home, QBCC, NSW Planning and ACCC sources and gives a practical supplier/installer document checklist. Product promotion must wait for the full research, exact-identity, safety, availability, approved tracking and permitted-image gates. Crazy Sales' potentially relevant airer/storage inventory overlaps existing guides, so advertiser approval alone does not justify a new retailer-led article.
