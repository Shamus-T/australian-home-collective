### Bosch SMV4HTX01A — dishwasher sizes guide

- [Australian manufacturer page](https://www.bosch-home.com.au/en/product/dishwashers/underbench-dishwashers/fully-integrated-dishwasher-60-cm/SMV4HTX01A), [specification and niche drawing](https://media3.bsh-group.com/Documents/specsheet/en-AU/SMV4HTX01A.pdf), [installation instructions](https://media3.bsh-group.com/Documents/9001999341_A.pdf) and [user manual](https://media3.bsh-group.com/Documents/9002017417_A.pdf).
- [Clean Good Guys listing](https://www.thegoodguys.com.au/bosch-series-4-60cm-fully-integrated-dishwasher-smv4htx01a), SKU 50076488.
- Adds a genuinely different fully integrated installation, not another freestanding colour. Fourteen place settings; 598mm body width, 815–875mm adjustable height and 550mm depth. The drawing specifies a 600mm niche width and at least 550mm depth; 1155mm door-open depth is not standing/loading clearance.
- Cabinet front is not supplied. Panel weight, plinth movement, fixings and services require installer checks. Condensation drying and ExtraDry do not guarantee dry plastic containers. Lower cutlery basket uses lower-rack space.
- All ten available individual owner comments checked. Cleaning, fit and quietness are recurring positives; a low-rating entry without text supplies no cause. Limited and sometimes incentivised evidence supports moderate confidence, not reliability claims. Retailer states two-year manufacturer warranty.

### Haier HRF510FHS — fridge dimensions guide

- [Australian manufacturer page](https://www.haier.com.au/refrigeration/refrigerators/509l-500-series-french-door-refrigerator-freezer-water-non-plumbed-hrf510fhs-62296.html), [planning sheet](https://dam.fisherpaykel.com/KZ3PKN00/at/wfbv32nrz786q58pqt6wkwn/Haier-SpecificationGuide-en-HRF510FS-HRF510FHS-HRF510FHC-FrenchDoorRefrigeratorFreezer-0-90004643B-AU-NZ.pdf) and [user guide](https://dam.fisherpaykel.com/KZ3PKN00/at/75nf5nmpb38tc5h35w47vrx/Haier-UserGuide-en-HRF510BHC-HRF510BW-HRF510BS-HRF510FHC-HRF510FHS-HRF510FS-HRF510BEC-Refrigeration-0-V2-NZ-AU.pdf).
- [Clean Good Guys listing](https://www.thegoodguys.com.au/haier-509l-french-door-refrigerator-hrf510fhs), SKU 50094832.
- Adds French doors with refillable water rather than a plumbing requirement. 509L total is split into 372L fresh-food and 137L freezer space. Body: 790W × 1725H × 707D mm. Published cavity: 850W × 1775H × 757D mm.
- Critical distinction: the page-33 French-door diagram specifies 30mm each side, 50mm top and rear. The adjacent bottom-mount diagram specifies 50mm sides. The model-specific planning drawing agrees with the French-door diagram. Do not apply the adjacent model’s requirement or assume body dimensions alone establish fit. Check supplied instructions and door/drawer access.
- Ten recent individual comments from 41 displayed reviews checked. Storage layout is useful, but water refilling and awkward ice-tray access appear in owner accounts. Manual ice and a smaller freezer are explicit compromises. Two-year parts/labour warranty; compressor extension is not a whole-appliance labour warranty. No long-term reliability claim.

### Instant Pot Duo Plus 8L 113-0063-01-AU — small kitchen appliances guide

- [Australian manufacturer page](https://www.instantpot.com.au/products/instant-pot-8l-duo-plus), [Duo Plus manual with Australian electrical specifications](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0727/7264/9209/files/Instant_Pot_Duo_Plus_User_Manual_1.pdf?v=1749536651), [clean Good Guys listing](https://www.thegoodguys.com.au/instant-8-litre-duo-plus-multi-cooker-113-0063-01-au), SKU 50098032.
- Adds larger-batch pressure/slow cooking alongside the existing 6L Breville. It does not air fry or replace the paused Ninja’s crisping functions. Australian model: 1200W, 6.8kg empty, 33.5 × 38 × 36.5cm. Eight litres is nominal bowl volume, not a safe cooking fill.
- Manual limits pressure cooking to two-thirds, or half for expanding/foaming food. Minimum thin liquid for the 8L model is 500mL unless the recipe specifies otherwise. Pressurising and release time are extra. Deglazing and release instructions matter; never force the lid.
- Read individual owner comments; duplicate author/text was not treated as independent evidence. Reports include burn-warning sensitivity and learning the settings. Retailer remote-control/WhisperQuiet wording was not adopted because it does not establish this exact model’s features. Australian 12-month warranty and local service route checked.

### De’Longhi TRRS0715EG — oil-column versus panel/convector guide

- [Clean Good Guys listing](https://www.thegoodguys.com.au/delonghi-1500w-radia-s-oil-column-heater-wtimer-trrs0715eg), SKU 50045856; [manufacturer-authored Australian/New Zealand manual hosted by an Australian retailer](https://www.appliancesonline.com.au/public/manuals/Delonghi-TRRS0715EG-Radia-S-Digital-Oil-Column-Heater-User-Manual.pdf).
- Adds a 1500W oil-column choice with digital thermostat/timer beside the existing 2400W convector. Lower maximum electrical power also means lower maximum heating output; this is not a like-for-like energy-efficiency advantage. Heat is gradual, not instant.
- Fan-free is not silent: owner reports mention expansion/cooling clicks and timer frustration. Casing gets hot. Keep upright and uncovered, use a suitable wall outlet, do not dry laundry on it or use an external timer. Retain the article’s stricter one-metre safety advice rather than reduce it to the manual’s minimum 50cm separation. Supervision and switching off before sleeping/leaving remain explicit.
- Ten recent individual comments from 143 displayed retailer reviews and limited exact-model independent owner material checked. Duplicate accounts are not independent evidence; unsupported pet/child safety assurances were rejected. Retailer states seven-year manufacturer warranty. Do not infer lifespan from review averages.
