## Purpose

Australian Home Collective helps Australians make informed home-planning and buying decisions. Recommendations begin with a household problem and are based on suitability, not a universal “best” claim.

Products are not selected merely because they are popular, expensive, highly rated or pay a higher commission. A guide should explain fit, access, installation, compatibility, usability, ownership considerations, support and material trade-offs before it presents a product.

## AHC Product Research

AHC Product Research is the required assessment process for named products that may receive promotional placement.

A research-supported assessment draws on evidence from:

- manufacturer specifications, manuals and written warranty documents;
- credible independent expert or technical reviews, where they add useful evidence;
- recurring themes across a meaningful body of owner feedback;
- Australian availability, warranty support, service access and fulfilment; and
- Australian regulator, warning and recall checks where relevant.

Manufacturer material may verify specifications but does not independently establish quality. A capable expert or technical review strengthens an assessment but is not a mandatory source category: shallow, poorly evidenced or irrelevant reviews should not determine the outcome. Retailer rankings, star averages, bestseller labels and review counts are not proof of suitability.

## Required reader-facing information

Every promoted product assessment must state:

- who the product may suit;
- who it may not suit;
- material drawbacks and recurring concerns;
- evidence confidence;
- the last-reviewed date; and
- whether the assessment is research-only or includes genuine hands-on testing.

Unless the published assessment explicitly describes what AHC tested and how, it must say that AHC has not tested the product firsthand.

## Hard exclusions

A product cannot receive promotional placement while any of these conditions applies:

- an active recall or unresolved safety concern;
- repeated serious reliability failures;
- poor or unclear Australian support or warranty arrangements;
- misleading or materially unverifiable claims;
- suspicious, overly dependent or insufficient evidence;
- an unsuitable seller, delivery or fulfilment route;
- low-confidence evidence; or
- a poor fit for the guide and intended reader.

Commission value cannot offset a hard exclusion.

## Research outcomes

Every completed assessment receives one outcome:

- **Research-supported option** — the evidence and suitability case meet the publication standard.
- **Promising, but evidence is limited** — the option may be useful, but evidence is not yet strong enough for promotion.
- **Mixed evidence — not promoted** — material concerns or conflicting evidence prevent promotion.
- **Does not meet our standard** — the product fails a required threshold or triggers a hard exclusion.

Only a **Research-supported option** may receive promotional affiliate placement. The structured record must also show:

- editorial status approved;
- evidence confidence high or moderate;
- recall and safety status clear;
- a current last-reviewed date;
- explicit approval for affiliate use;
- material drawbacks and suitability statements; and
- the required source records.

## Review interval and new evidence

Approved product records expire 180 days after the last-reviewed date. The product must be re-reviewed before it can continue to render after that date.

A review happens sooner when material evidence emerges, including a recall, safety warning, specification change, support change, recurring reliability pattern, seller or fulfilment change, or product replacement. A product may be downgraded, paused or removed at any time.

## Commercial placement controls

Affiliate links are active and are disclosed site-wide and near promotional placements. A commercial link may appear only through the central product registry and reusable component.

The build must reject:

- an affiliate URL with a missing or incorrect configured tracking value;
- a commercial link without the required sponsored, nofollow and safe-target attributes;
- an unapproved product or placement;
- a record missing required research fields;
- an approved product with an active recall or unresolved safety flag;
- an expired approved review; and
- pages missing the site-wide disclosure.

Commercial relationships do not determine methodology or conclusions. The surrounding guidance must remain useful if the reader does not use an affiliate link.

## Ratings, scores and testing claims

AHC does not add retailer prices, star ratings, review scores, bestseller claims or unexplained numerical rankings to promotional placements. A product is not described as tested, owned or inspected unless that is true and the testing notes are recorded.

## Continuous improvement

We correct and update research when evidence, guidance, standards or reader needs change. The guiding question remains:

> **Will this help someone make a better decision for their home?**
