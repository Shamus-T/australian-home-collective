# Robot lawn mower guide: editorial and monetisation assessment

Reviewed 13 September 2026 against main commit
`868bad00cfa5ac7072c9e8b38b2d6a6c985dcd3a`.

## Reader decision and scope

Help readers establish whether automated mowing suits their actual lawn, access,
grass height, available operating hours and maintenance expectations before buying.
The existing lawn-care guide addresses lawn condition; it does not replace this
installation and ownership assessment. New route:
`/guides/robot-lawn-mower-buying-guide-australia/`.

Publication edit follows the Editorial Style Guide, Brand Style Guide,
Recommendation Methodology and Publishing Standards Manual. Australian English;
approximately 1,010 body words; no rankings, prices, invented tests or numerical
installation limits. The slope example is arithmetic, not a mower specification.

## Sources checked

Manufacturer documentation was used internally for technical verification. The
public article has only official safety links, following the owner's direction.
No named model recommendations appear in the article.

- [Husqvarna AU work-area capacity explanation](https://www.husqvarna.com/au/support/husqvarna-self-service/understanding-the-work-area-capacity-and-maximum-cutting-time-of-your-automower-robotic-lawn-mower-ka-01508/): capacity assumptions and distinction between scheduled operating time, cutting, searching and charging. The article asks readers to verify the exact model's schedule rather than applying this manufacturer's limits to all mowers.
- [Husqvarna AU robotic mower FAQ](https://www.husqvarna.com/au/learn-and-discover/robotic-mower-faq/): frequent small cuts, clippings, remaining trimming and manual movement between fragmented lawns. Marketing claims of guaranteed containment, universal suitability or negligible work were not adopted.
- [Husqvarna AU 305 specifications](https://www.husqvarna.com/au/robotic-lawn-mowers/automower-305/): a concrete example of different boundary and interior slope limits, cutting-height range, physical wire and absence of object detection. No model-specific limits are reproduced as universal guidance.
- [Current 305 / 310 Mark II / 315 Mark II manual](https://www-static-nw.husqvarna.com/hbd/tdrdownload/v2/pub000105646/doc000264809/OM/HnJEPn_7AyaXl2l4uicasvr5ZuI), revision 1462-009, 16 March 2026, linked by the Australian support page: blade hazards, shutdown before maintenance, standing water, installation planning and differing weather ratings. The guide uses a conservative separate mowing window for children and pets; no universal clearance values or imported electrical requirements.
- [Husqvarna AU satellite and internet troubleshooting](https://www.husqvarna.com/au/support/husqvarna-self-service/why-does-automower-keep-stopping-epos-wire-free-signal-issues-ka-70353/): trees/buildings, correction data, internet versus reference-station arrangements. Connectivity requirements are expressly model dependent.
- [Dreame Australia A1 Pro](https://dreame.com.au/products/dreame-roboticmower-a1-pro): confirms LiDAR navigation without boundary wire or RTK station; supports distinguishing technologies rather than equating every wireless mower with satellites. No marketing accuracy or obstacle-safety claim adopted.
- [ACCC Product Safety recalls](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/recalls): public recall search. Direct HTTP retrieval succeeded even though the research browser's text fetch returned an error. Reader directed to check exact model and serial number; the guide makes no recall-clear claim.
- [Fire and Rescue NSW battery guidance](https://www.fire.nsw.gov.au/fire-safety/home-fire-safety/battery-and-charging-safety/shop-charge-and-recycle-safely): official purchasing, charging and disposal precautions. No generic advice to alter a mower's designed charging cycle.

The worksheet records actual dimensions separately from manufacturer requirements.
Neither it nor the illustration provides installation or safety approval. The
illustration is original SVG and the worksheet uses the existing AHC logo.

## Amazon AU investigation and decision

**Decision: publish the guide without affiliate placements.** Promising candidates
were found, but two products have not completed the evidence threshold required
for an enabled guide. No commercial record was approved and no tracking link
was inserted. This is an evidence limitation, not a claim that the products are
unsafe or that Amazon AU does not stock robot mowers.

Direct Amazon page text was retrieved on 13 September 2026. HTML was parsed with
script/style content excluded so generic embedded availability strings were not
mistaken for visible offer evidence. Offer availability was shown for Sydney 2000;
no order was placed and no claim of delivery to every postcode is made.

Owner-evidence checks:

- [ProductReview's 305 listing](https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/husqvarna-automower-230acx-220ac-210c-305) has only four reviews and includes purchases from 2013 and 2016, with an explicit three-wheel description. It must not be used to transfer reliability or support conclusions to the current four-wheel model. One later positive review does not establish a recurring body of evidence.
- [R4 owner discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/automower/comments/1b184yp/aspire_r4/) includes one Australian R4 owner's positive report and follow-up, but other detailed reliability comments concern a 315X and a Landroid. Those are not evidence about the R4.
- [CHOICE R4 review page](https://www.choice.com.au/products/outdoor/gardening/lawnmowers-and-trimmers/husqvarna-aspire-r4) was located; the existence of a review was not treated as a positive recommendation or as access to the full test result.
- Manufacturer warranty information was located at [Husqvarna AU warranty](https://www.husqvarna.com/au/support/warranty/), but no completed product-specific support approval is asserted.

Revisit with enough current-generation owner evidence, relevant expert findings,
written Australian support terms, exact model/serial recall checks and same-date
Amazon offer validation. Only then add two qualifying products through the central
catalogue and reusable disclosed commercial component using AHC's configured
Amazon tracking. Do not add generic Amazon search links or unrelated filler items.

existing lawn-care guide. PDF linked at the introduction and the final checklist.

images or JavaScript page errors; both PDF links present; category/index discovery
verified. PDF has one A4 page and one embedded AHC logo image. The article also
shows the yard illustration within the body so its labels are readable outside
the shared header crop. Search-result title shortened to meet the 60-character gate.
