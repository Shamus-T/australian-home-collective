# LG Fit & Max first look — editorial and commercial evidence

Reviewed 10 October 2026. Article: `/guides/lg-fit-max-436l-fridge-first-look/`.

## Firsthand basis

The author confirmed seeing this fridge hands-on. The features that stood out were the zero clearance hinges and dedicated fast door cooling. No venue, measured cooling time, acoustic testing, energy testing, ownership period or reliability findings were supplied. The article does not invent any of these. Commercial records remain research-only because a hands-on viewing is not performance testing.

All four Australian product pages were retrieved and specifications checked. Shared specifications: 436 L total, 308 L fridge, 128 L freezer; 697 W x 1740 H x 696 D mm; packed 739 x 1812 x 772 mm; 71 kg unpacked / 78 kg packed; 5.5 stars, 261 kWh/year; 4 mm side clearance recommended. Side-opening width of 705 mm is AHC arithmetic (697 + 4 + 4), not a supplied certified cavity dimension. The author subsequently confirmed 4 mm side clearance, 9 mm top clearance, front vent/exhaust and slim doors from the hands-on context. Article attributes the 9 mm top figure to those hands-on notes rather than claiming it appears in the manual. Nominal opening height 1749 mm is AHC arithmetic (1740 + 9). The manual rear allowance remains separately identified.

Manual page 11 was text-extracted and visually reviewed. It specifies more than 50 mm behind the appliance. Its door-opening drawing shows approximate envelope dimensions F 1353 and G 918 mm; article avoids implying these remove the need to check the actual wall and cabinetry arrangement. LG's online dimension image also shows a rear gap of at least 50 mm. The article uses the manual's stricter wording. Page 12 calls for reversal by qualified LG Electronics personnel. Shared optional manual features are not treated as included.

LG's DoorCooling+ footnote says cooling starts 15 seconds after the doors are closed. No measured speed comparison is made. The outlet image was inspected. The Australian specification tables list no folding shelf, wine rack, Fresh Converter, dispenser or automatic ice maker. Shared marketing gallery imagery is inconsistent with some of those exclusions, so the article flags the issue and follows the model specification tables.

Illustrative running costs: 261 x AUD 0.30 = AUD 78.30/year; 261 x AUD 0.40 = AUD 104.40/year. Not measured costs or a current electricity tariff quote. LG lists connected-product security support through 31 December 2031. Published warranty structure: 2 years product parts/labour, 3 additional years sealed-system parts, then 5 compressor-part-only years. No blanket ten-year repair promise.
