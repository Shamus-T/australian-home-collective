The current official product specification distinguishes:

| Cabinet width | 693 |
| Cabinet height | 1718 |

The handles are integrated. Use 723 mm as the closed product depth, including the door, rather than treating the 641 mm cabinet depth as the complete appliance. The official dimension drawing places the depth arrow across the product through the door front. The 1360 mm door-open figure starts at the product rear, not the wall; rear ventilation and a person's standing space remain separate checks.

## Official dimension and installation sheet

Direct source: https://www.westinghouse.com.au/documenthandler.ashx?file=aHR0cHM6Ly9yZXNvdXJjZS5lbGVjdHJvbHV4LmNvbS5hdS9QdWJsaWMvRmlsZS8_SWQ9NjM0Mjk1&lang=

Title: Large Top Mount Fridges, Dimension and installation guide. One page. Footer: WHS_Dim_LargeTM_Nov24, copyright 2024. Still linked from the current product page on the check date.

The sheet expressly includes WTM4302WD. Its table confirms 699 W x 1725 H x 723 D, and 1360 mm depth at a 90-degree door opening. It shows 30 mm at each side, 50 mm behind, and 90 mm above. Doors project beyond adjacent cupboards. In a corner, its X dimension is 430 mm on the hinge side, allowing the shelves to be removed. These are different measurements: ordinary ventilation space does not establish enough room beside a wall to remove shelves.

## Current manufacturer user manual

Direct source: https://www.westinghouse.com.au/documenthandler.ashx?file=aHR0cHM6Ly9yZXNvdXJjZS5lbGVjdHJvbHV4LmNvbS5hdS9QdWJsaWMvRmlsZS8_SWQ9NjMxNzY1&lang=

24 PDF pages. Cover includes WTM4302; printed page 6 identifies WTM4302WD/SD. Printed page 11 / PDF page 11 contains the relevant installation diagrams and corner-clearance table.

Page 11 specifies 30 mm minimum at each side and 50 mm behind. The minimum above the hinge cover is 10 mm only where the cupboard allows airflow as illustrated; otherwise the recommended top space increases to 90 mm. It specifies 430 mm minimum on the hinge side for WTM4302 models positioned in a corner, to permit bin and shelf removal. Do not mistake that for the ordinary side ventilation gap. Its levelling procedure tilts the front back; a close-height installation must also account for final levelling. The page separately limits rear spacing to 75 mm where the sides and rear are not enclosed by cabinetry.

## Publication guidance

Suggested concise example: "The WTM4302WD-R is 699 mm wide, 1725 mm high and 723 mm deep with the door shut. Its drawing allows 30 mm on each side and 50 mm behind, with 90 mm above in the standard arrangement. Beside a wall on the hinge side, it needs 430 mm so you can open the door far enough to remove bins and shelves. The doors sit in front of the cupboards."

Keep the top-clearance exception in a linked source note or a separate explanatory sentence if used; never publish 10 mm without its airflow condition. Do not invent a single minimum-cavity W x H x D from these figures. In particular, the product and cabinet measurements have different reference points, and the doors are designed to project.
