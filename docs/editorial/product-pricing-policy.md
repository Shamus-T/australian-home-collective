# AHC product pricing rule

Effective 11 October 2026. Applies to product cards and product-specific prices in articles.

- Do not display RRP, crossed-out prices, was/now pricing, calculated savings, or wording implying a discount against a reference price.
- Optionally report an actual Australian manufacturer-direct selling price. Verify the exact model, finish, currency and direct purchase offer on the official Australian manufacturer website. An RRP or a price quoted for another retailer is not a manufacturer-direct selling price.
- Record amount, AUD currency, manufacturer, official source URL, verification date, next review date and supporting evidence in `manufacturerPrice`. Keep source URLs in editorial records; do not add promotional manufacturer links to guides.
- Display a modest dated attribution: “Listed on [manufacturer]’s website, checked [date].” Explain that this is a manufacturer-direct product price, delivery may apply, and retailer prices and availability may differ.
- Use “Check current retailer prices and availability” above retailer links. Preserve affiliate disclosures.
- Review all published manufacturer prices on the first of each month, starting 1 November 2026. Verification must not be more than 31 days apart. Recheck the official price and exact variant; update evidence and dates only after successful verification. Remove the price if unavailable, ambiguous, RRP-only or unverifiable.
- Builds reject overdue/invalid price records. The renderer also omits overdue prices on subsequent builds. A static deployed page cannot change itself at midnight; the monthly review must publish the correction/removal. Build checks validate records, not live manufacturer websites.

ACCC reference: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/pricing/price-displays . Accurate attribution is not a guarantee that a price presentation cannot mislead; do not imply retailer savings or describe this rule as ACCC approval.
