## Editorial comparison approach
Segment recommendations by room and use case: sunlit living room, controlled-light cinema, mixed family viewing, gaming, accessible dialogue and value. Avoid unsupported best-of labels. Explain OLED, QLED, Mini LED and RGB LED correctly: QLED is a quantum-dot colour technology, not a mutually exclusive backlight class.
