# Rangehood buying guide: editorial and source record

Prepared and reviewed 12 September 2026 against repository main commit
`d1662358a792862b459b5b528e2a9e22ebf946fd`.

## Reader decision and duplicate check

Help a homeowner compare rangehood arrangements after checking cooktop
coverage, cabinetry, air route, noise and maintenance. The current guide tree
has no dedicated rangehood or kitchen-extraction guide. Existing condensation
and appliance-fit pages are complementary. Kitchen and Guides index entries
place this guide immediately after the dishwasher guide.

## Evidence checked

- [Bosch Australia rangehood buying guide](https://www.bosch-home.com.au/products/cooking-baking/rangehoods/buying-guide): operating arrangements, formats, width/coverage and filter distinctions. No generic clearance numbers, performance percentages or “quiet” thresholds reproduced.
- [Schweigen buying guide](https://www.schweigen.com.au/get-inspired-3/silent-rangehood-buying-guide): system-specific acoustic ducting and the distinction between motor noise and airflow noise. Marketing superiority, health and efficiency claims were not adopted. Its duct advice is expressly not generalised to other systems.
- [Fisher & Paykel Box Wall installation guide, US/CA](https://www.fisherpaykel.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-fpa-master-catalog/default/dwfb349ef1/InstallationManuals-FisherPaykelUS/FP-InstallGuide-en-HC24DCXB4-HC30DCXB4-HC36DCXB4-BoxWallRangeHood-0-106075B-US-CA.pdf): printed page 11 (PDF page 11) explicitly states that recirculation does not remove moisture; page 2 notes room ventilation around fuel-burning appliances. Used for physical principles only. No overseas clearance, electrical or code requirements imported. The public link explains this limit.
- [Chef Australia filter-care information](https://www.chefappliances.com.au/rangehoods/canopy/crc612sb/): a factual example that some removable mesh filters are dishwasher safe. No model recommendation or retailer link. Actual filter-care instructions govern.

Manufacturer sources were opened, not accepted solely from search snippets.
A preliminary Fisher & Paykel AU specification PDF was excluded as an
installation source because it explicitly says it is preliminary and not for
installation. Unretrievable regulator pages were not cited or treated as
verified law. The guide does not state numerical clearances or interpret codes;
it directs readers to Australian manuals and the installer for those decisions.

## Cooktop-type revision — 13 September 2026

Added induction, gas and radiant-electric selection context, including rapid
boiling, condensation, capture area, usable airflow and the need to reassess
an existing rangehood when changing cooktop type. The guide stays below 1,200
words. Publication edit retains the qualified installer and gasfitter advice.

Evidence opened and reviewed:
- https://www.schweigen.com.au/article/tips-to-reduce-condensation-when-cooking-with-induction-cooktops — rapid heating, vapour condensing on cold hood surfaces, power settings, hood coverage and system-specific operating guidance. This source does not establish a universal higher extraction rate for induction than gas. Its specific fan-speed and timing advice is not generalised to other systems.
- https://www.yourhome.gov.au/energy/appliances — induction heating and responsiveness.
- https://www.yourhome.gov.au/live-adapt/indoor-air-quality — cooking and combustion pollutants and ventilation to outdoors.

Editorial judgement: cooktop type and cooking load influence rangehood choice;
a larger advertised airflow alone does not establish steam capture or prevent
condensation. No universal induction airflow value or gas-to-induction multiplier
is asserted. Manufacturer references are internal evidence only. The public
article retains only the official Energy Safe Victoria and Fire and Rescue NSW
safety links added after the initial publication.
