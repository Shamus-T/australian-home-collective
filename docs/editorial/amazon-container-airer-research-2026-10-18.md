## Joseph Joseph: why this set, and its limits

- [Exact Australian manufacturer page](https://josephjoseph.com.au/products/nest-lock-5-piece-leakproof-container-set-multicolour): 230 mL, 540 mL, 1.1 L, 1.85 L and 3 L with matching lids. Five containers plus five lids is sometimes called ten pieces; it is not ten containers. Published set dimensions are H24.5 × W16.3 × D20 cm.
- Useful for varied leftovers and empty-container organisation, not five identical meal-prep portions. Bases nest; lids snap together in a separate stack. Australian Multicolour/Multi owner comments support the lid-matching and nesting use case; an April 2022 owner specifically explains separate lid storage.
- Freezer and top-rack dishwasher use are permitted. Microwave with the lid removed and resting loosely on top, never fastened. Avoid overheating foods high in oil, fat, sugar or strong colourants because staining/pitting can occur. No oven or grill use.
- Manufacturer-hosted reviews are pooled across configurations. Difficult lid separation and a staining complaint are range-level anecdotes, not exact-model failure rates or a safety diagnosis. Firm clips can be unsuitable for limited grip. No AHC leak test or longevity test was performed.
- The US multicolour B07G92P81W/81098 and sky-blue B07Z5MX4V2/81108 were also orderable but add no distinct editorial role. The blue ASIN must not be misidentified as AU SKU 81105. Selected 81081 aligns the Australian documentation, GTIN and manufacturer image.
- Exact-model manufacturer photograph sourced from Joseph Joseph, with provenance in the catalogue. No Amazon product photograph copied or hotlinked.

## Brabantia: resolve the misleading dimensions

[Exact 403408 manufacturer page](https://www.brabantia.com/uk/hangon-drying-rack-20-metres-matt-black/) confirms 20 m line length, steel construction, 4.6 kg frame weight, six hanger hooks, adjustable wings, transport lock and a lock against accidental folding. **98 × 59 × 5 cm is folded**, not the open drying footprint. The Amazon 13 kg load claim was not confirmed in exact-model manufacturer guidance and is not adopted as an established capacity.

Outdoor use is limited: no rain or corrosive conditions. The product should not be presented as a permanent exposed-balcony clothesline. The brand offers some component spares, but UK warranty terms do not establish Amazon AU warranty handling. The Australian manufacturer page inspected describes the white family variant, so it does not override exact black-model documentation.

Exact 20 m Matt Black overseas reviews describe stability and ease of use. Australian 25 m comments about wind/plastic joints are another size and not treated as exact 20 m evidence. There is no adequate controlled evidence of superiority to Pacifica. The exact PDF could not be fetched because of certificate validation; no certificate checks were disabled. The accessible exact-model manufacturer HTML supports the published claims.
