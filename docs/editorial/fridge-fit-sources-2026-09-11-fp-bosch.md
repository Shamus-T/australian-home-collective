### Verified figures and page references

- Page 12: overall 905 W x 1900 H x 748 D (depth excludes handles; this model has recessed handles). Door-open depth at 90 degrees from rear is 1142; the extra distance measured forward from the closed door is 394. The narrower cabinet-only height is 1867, not the overall appliance height. Product dimensions may vary by +/- 2 mm.
- Pages 13–14: proud installation, full door rotation: cavity 945 W x 1920 H x 690 D, with separate 350 door clearance in the drawing. For the illustrated 90-degree limited opening arrangement, cavity width is 985. Full drawer/shelf access needs doors past 90 degrees.
- Pages 15–16: flush installation, full door rotation: cavity 1125 W x 1945 H x 778 D, with separate 260 door clearance in the drawing. The illustrated 90-degree arrangement instead specifies 1075 cavity width.
- Pages 14 and 16: 30 rear clearance, plus a 50 vent above the refrigerator cabinet or around the top of the overhead cupboard. This vent is not the same as a simple top-of-fridge-to-cupboard gap. Cavity height includes a 10 mm feet increase.

### Editorial interpretation

For concise guide copy, use the proud/full-rotation cavity example and explain that doors project in front of cabinetry. Link pages 13–16 so readers with deep side walls or flush cabinetry see the different layout. Do not imply that 945 x 1920 x 690 alone establishes fit; the side-wall/door swing and ventilation drawings remain part of the installation. Do not add 748 product depth to a generic rear gap to invent the proud cavity depth. The current product page lists 945 x 1920 x 690 but does not explain the configuration as clearly as the installation guide.

### Verified figures and page references

- Specification sheet pages 1–2: 600 W x 1860 H x 660 D; integrated vertical handle; reversible door hinge.
- Specification sheet page 3 inner-handle row: cabinet/body depth 590; closed-door depth 660; depth with door open 1210; width with door at 90 degrees 600. The outer-handle row differs and must not be used for this model. Web extracted text for the inner-handle 90-degree width was wrong; visual table says 600.
- Specification sheet page 3 plan drawing: 65 mm at the integrated-handle side for easy hand access, 20 mm on the hinge side for the shown 125-degree layout, with the body/cabinetry relation shown as 590. These are configuration-specific clearances, not a complete cavity W x H x D specification.
- Current user manual page 34: keep a small gap beside the appliance and leave external ventilation openings clear; page 35 sends installer to the enclosed fitting instructions. No complete universal cavity height/depth was established in this research.

### Editorial interpretation

Useful compact example: a 590 mm cabinet depth becomes 660 mm with the door closed and 1210 mm with it open. Bosch's Perfect Fit description does not justify saying the appliance fits any 600 mm opening or can always be pushed against a wall: handle access, hinge swing and ventilation need the exact drawing. Do not invent a cavity width by adding the diagram's side gaps unless all layout conditions are stated.
