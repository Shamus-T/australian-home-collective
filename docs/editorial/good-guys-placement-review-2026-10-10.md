## Affiliate and availability checks

Each configured Partnerize link was navigated in the browser. It reached the correct Good Guys model page with clickref retained. Exact model, normal retailer seller and enabled Add to cart were checked. Availability is recorded as available-to-order: delivery, collection, installation services and charges still depend on postcode. No purchase or checkout was completed. Two-year Australian manufacturer warranty/support routes were checked. Conditional extended-warranty promotions are excluded. Product images came from the public exact-model retailer listings and are credited as sourced from The Good Guys website, not as supplied directly to AHC.

Search-index checks restricted to productsafety.gov.au found no matching notices for the assessed exact models/families. This is not represented as a complete direct-register inspection or a safety certification. AHC makes no new safety/compliance performance claims in these placements.
